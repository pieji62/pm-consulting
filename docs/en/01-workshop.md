# 1. What is AI Website Workshop?

A folder of tools and guides for creating information websites with an agent.
The result is yours: files you can keep and move.

1. Describe your business in everyday language.
2. Review what the agent understood and its page proposal.
3. Preview the site on your computer and request adjustments.
4. Read the verification report.
5. Decide whether to publish. Publication needs external accounts and your permission.

**Green:** public pages, text, images and content. **Yellow:** a request needs
assessment, such as a booking service. **Red:** outside scope, such as customer
accounts. These are workshop boundaries, not judgments about all technologies.

A restaurant menu is Green; processing payments is not. If the agent stops, you
have done nothing wrong: it is identifying where you need help.

# 15. Prepare an Azure account

Azure is Microsoft's service for serving site files on the Internet.
An account and subscription do not mean everything is free.

1. Visit azure.microsoft.com and review the available offer's terms.
2. Create or use your account personally. Complete identity, payment and verification
   only on official pages; never share those details with the agent.
3. Open portal.azure.com and confirm the selected subscription.
4. Review Cost Management + Billing and configure budget alerts where supported.
   A budget alert does not automatically prevent spending.
5. Explicitly confirm the resources and plan before creating anything.

The seminar proposes Static Web Apps Free. It has quotas and different guarantees
from a production plan. Microsoft positions it for personal/learning projects.
Businesses must assess availability, support, traffic and requirements first.
See [official plans](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/static-web-apps/plans).

If you cannot create a subscription, use the instructor's local demonstration.
Do not buy another product or switch to a paid plan merely to continue.

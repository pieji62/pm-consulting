# 11. Understand the agent's questions

Questions prevent invented business details. "Audience" means who should visit the
site. "Functionality" means what people will be able to do there.

1. Describe your customers, for example "families in my neighborhood."
2. Say what they need to find: menu, services, hours or work samples.
3. Distinguish displaying from processing: showing a phone number differs from
   storing information submitted through a form.
4. Confirm which images/text you may use and who reviewed them.
5. If a fact is unknown, answer "pending." Reject invented hours or prices.

"I want visitors to book" is ambiguous. The agent must ask whether you mean showing
a phone number or integrating a service, rather than deciding silently.
For technical wording, ask: "Explain it using an example from my business."

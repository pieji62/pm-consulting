# 7. Protect your account with a second step

Two-factor authentication adds a check beyond your password.

1. In GitHub, open your profile picture > Settings.
2. Under Password and authentication, find Two-factor authentication.
3. Choose Enable two-factor authentication and configure an authenticator or another
   supported option you understand. Scan the code yourself and never share it.
4. Store recovery codes securely outside the repository.
5. Check that you can sign in before the seminar. Protect your email account too.

If you lose your phone, use official recovery and your codes. Neither the instructor
nor the agent should ask for codes or disable protection to continue.

# 20. When to get professional help

Get help for customer accounts, sensitive data, payment collection, databases,
integrations or important legal/availability needs. Also get help when two repair
attempts cannot resolve a mandatory check.

1. Say: "Generate a professional handover; do not send my information to anyone."
2. The agent runs npm.cmd start -- handover . inside the site.
3. Review .workshop/engineering-brief.json: objective, functionality, boundary
   reasons, architecture, security, privacy, integrations and open questions.
4. You may voluntarily share it with any qualified provider. Remove information
   you do not wish to share and agree scope, costs and maintenance.
5. Keep the information site separate from new functionality pending assessment.

Yellow does not mean safe if we insist. Red does not mean the technology is always
dangerous: this product does not implement it. Professional help is neither
contracted automatically nor a commercial contact created by the workshop.
